Page Pacer

Read a PDF one half-page at a time, on a timer. It moves to the next part by itself, so you keep a steady speed through every reading. Turn the 🖍 pen on and highlight words while the timer keeps running.

Or drop a PDF here. The file stays in this browser and is never uploaded.

Your PDFs

Backup of your progress

The file keeps your place, ★ marks, 🖍 highlights and reading times for every PDF (not the PDFs themselves). Keep it in Google Drive or WhatsApp to yourself. Load it on any phone or laptop, or if this browser ever loses your progress.

How it works

  1. Each page is cut into 2 parts (you can choose 1–4). The cut falls in a gap between lines, so no line is split.
  2. Empty page margins are cropped, so the text shows bigger.
  3. Set the seconds per part. When time runs out, the next part opens.
  4. Press 🖍 to turn the pen on. It stays on while you read. Tap a word, or drag across words; tap again to remove. The timer stops only while your finger (or mouse) is pressed on a word. If the part was about to change, you get 3 more seconds after you lift your finger. Press 🖍 again to put the pen away.
  5. ⚙ More → 📄 Download highlighted PDF gives a copy with real highlights. Open it in Revision Cards and your highlighted lines become flashcards.
  6. Mark hard pages with ★. After a full reading, review only the marked pages.
  7. Each full reading is saved (Reading 1, 2, 3…) with the time it took.
